What this does

Off by default, and worth turning on. These public sources help PeriodiCool recognize your magazines and their editions, check their frequency, and list past issues.

  • Only an ISSN (or a title when there is none) is sent, straight from this PeriodiCool to the source.
  • Answers are kept in your database for 30 days. Nothing changes until you apply it in a magazine's Metadata pop-up.
  • Other names found for magazines with an ISSN also help recognize files in the library. Searches and grabs use only your own titles.
  • Only metadata is read. No issues or files are ever downloaded from these sources.
